BinLink — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

BinLink ("we", "us") provides on-demand waste-pickup services in Ghana through two mobile apps: BinLink Eco (for households) and BinLink Collector (for collectors). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number and email address.
  • Location: your pickup address and, during an active pickup, live location — used to dispatch and let households track collectors in real time.
  • Pickup data: bookings, bin sizes, photos you attach, schedules, prices agreed and status updates.
  • Payment data: mobile-money payments are processed by Paystack. We receive a transaction reference and status; we do not store your full mobile-money PIN or wallet credentials.
  • Device data: a push-notification token and basic device information, for notifications and reliability.

How we use it

  • To create and manage bookings and match households with collectors.
  • To show live tracking, ETAs and pickup status.
  • To process payments and send receipts.
  • To send pickup, payment and account notifications.
  • To keep the service secure and improve it.

Who we share it with

  • The other party to a pickup (a household and its assigned collector see each other's name, phone and the pickup location to complete the job).
  • Service providers that run the platform: Paystack (payments), Google Firebase (sign-in and notifications), and Supabase (secure data storage/hosting).
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Data retention & security

We keep your data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and access is restricted.

Your choices

  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and data at any time.
  • You can turn off location permission in your device settings (some features, like live tracking, will not work).
  • You can disable push notifications in your device settings.

Children

BinLink is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
